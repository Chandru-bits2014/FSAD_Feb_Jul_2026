State Management/Session Management

StateMgmt is a process by which one can manage and maintain the information about the client

State mgmt becomes critical when the client information becomes crucial for eg.in banking application one may have to maintain the pin number of a client and corresponding account number for the entire session across the web pages and have to process all data only wrt the said acct number,

(session: is a period interval between the connection and disconnection to the site by the user, many times it could be interval between  login and logout )

Therefore maintaining state information becomes critical and essential

Normally 
there are 2 types of facilities available for state management
a) Client side options
 namely
   -----Cookies
   -----Query String(URL rewriting)
   -----Hidden Control
   -----View State property
b) Server Side options
  ------Session Object
  ------Application Object
  ------ SQL Server
Many options could be discussed when a partivular server side technology is being learnt

Cookies:Cookies are chunks of data in the form of strings which could be generated on the server side and could be sent to the clients .

Cookies could be used to maintain client information 

Two types of cookies are 
a) Persistent cookie(Permanent)
b)Non Persistent Cookie(Temporary)
Every time a cookie is generated in the domain(site) it would be sent along with the response to the client and the cookie resides in the hard disc of the client, 
Next time when the request reaches the same domain from the same system the request would get attached with the cookie wrt that domain automatically
Server would have the facility to fetch the cookie from the request and manipulate it.
One site may produce any Number of cookies
But one client can store max of 20 cookies wrt a site and 1 cookie could store 4Kb
Persistent cookies are those which have a particular expiry date after the time lapses the cookie dies non persistent cookie would be alive only for that session
Mostly cookies could be used for sending small information in the form of advertisements etc.,
Not always cookies carry virus, not all pop ups are cookies, not all ads are cookies.
